# Location Design

## Rule

Locations are not cosmetic biomes. Each one must introduce a distinct pressure structure while reusing the same core engine: Role, Agenda, NPC routines, truth/evidence, Leverage, Suspicion, Exposure, Edges, Assets, Stakes, and Resolution.

## 1. Hotel

**Primary pressure:** access and routine.

Key systems:
- rooms and keys;
- check-in/check-out records;
- staff-only/service areas;
- housekeeping/maintenance schedules;
- elevators/stairs;
- guest requests and desk coverage;
- security cameras and access logs.

Natural Resolution: dawn, manager arrival, police arrival, or shift handoff.

Target full run: 25–35 min.

Prototype location and MVCR target.

## 2. Bullet Train

**Primary pressure:** departures and linear geography.

Key systems:
- train cars as a readable linear map;
- assigned seats/berths;
- staff routes;
- luggage and dining/service movement;
- station countdowns;
- passengers boarding/leaving;
- evidence/suspects becoming permanently unavailable after stops.

Natural Resolution: final station or required destination.

Target full run: 20–30 min.

Critical architecture test: should reuse the core simulation rather than require a hotel-specific rewrite.

## 3. Estate

**Primary pressure:** relationships and social history.

Key systems:
- family/employee relationship graph;
- invitations and uninvited guests;
- inheritance/business ties;
- dinner/event schedule;
- private rooms and public spaces;
- staff knowledge vs guest knowledge;
- long-running grudges and secrets.

Natural Resolution: dinner, will reading, wedding/reunion climax, departure, or formal announcement.

Target full run: 30–40 min.

## 4. Airplane

**Primary pressure:** confinement and manifests.

Key systems:
- assigned seats;
- cabin zones;
- passenger manifest;
- carry-on/checked luggage abstractions;
- crew/service routines;
- turbulence/seatbelt sign forcing temporary immobility;
- very limited exits and movement paths;
- arrival as a hard clock.

Natural Resolution: landing.

Target full run: 20–30 min.

## 5. Prison

**Primary pressure:** routine, institutional access, favors, and reputation.

Key systems:
- count/breakfast/work/yard/visit/dinner/lockdown schedule;
- inmate/guard/staff social groups;
- contraband as information/access rather than combat loot;
- visitation and transfer records;
- favors/Markers especially important;
- routine deviation highly visible.

Possible player Roles: inmate, guard, counselor, visitor, contractor, investigator.

Natural Resolution: lockdown, transfer, hearing, release window, or institutional investigation.

Target full run: 30–40 min.

## 6. Courthouse / Jury

**Primary pressure:** restricted information and judgment.

Core human Role for multiplayer: Juror.

Key systems:
- admissible evidence vs private knowledge;
- testimony and contradictions;
- recess interactions;
- jurors receiving different private context;
- improper outside contact;
- social influence during deliberation;
- personal bias/experience represented through information access or Edge interactions rather than simplistic hidden stat labels.

In multiplayer, each juror can begin with one piece of private information/context. Judge, attorneys, witnesses, and most court staff remain NPCs initially.

Natural Resolution: deliberation and verdict.

Target run: 25–40 min.

## Future candidate locations

Potential later environments should be evaluated for a unique pressure mechanic:
- film/television set — live production or shooting schedule;
- cruise ship — larger moving social environment with port calls;
- hospital — access, privacy, shifts, visitors, records;
- conference/convention — identity badges, sessions, networking;
- funeral/wake — inheritance, grief, relationships, short event window;
- remote research station — isolation and constrained communications;
- luxury resort — staff/guest class boundaries and scheduled activities.

A new Location is justified only if it changes how players reason and manage pressure, not because it looks cool.
