# Leverage, Suspicion & Exposure

## System goals

These three systems create the in-run economy and pressure curve.

- **Leverage** answers: What can I persuade/manipulate/access because of what I have done or learned?
- **Suspicion** answers: What does this particular person currently think about me?
- **Exposure** answers: How broadly dangerous/visible has my behavior become in the current run?

They must remain distinct.

---

## Leverage

Leverage is fungible, temporary, and lost at run end.

It represents social capital, useful secrets, credibility, favors, bargaining position, and influence. It is not money unless a specific contextual interaction explicitly treats it that way.

### Golden rule

**Leverage can buy opportunity, cooperation, access, or interpretation. It cannot buy truth.**

Never offer: `Spend 5 Leverage — Reveal culprit.`

Valid uses include:
- convince an NPC to answer a question;
- get restricted-but-plausible information;
- obtain temporary access;
- ask a coworker to cover a duty;
- call in a service/favor;
- obtain an Asset;
- delay a consequence briefly;
- gain an introduction;
- repair a damaged relationship;
- present a plausible explanation after suspicious behavior;
- bend a location rule when a believable human exception exists.

### Prototype economy

Start: **1 Leverage**.

Typical earnings:
- meaningful Role duty: +1;
- meaningful favor: +1;
- optional risk objective: +2;
- contradiction/evidence interaction with qualifying Edge: +1;
- high-Exposure Gambler bonus: +1 where specified.

Typical spends:
- minor cooperation: 1;
- useful information / favor: 2;
- meaningful access/intervention: 3;
- major exception: 4–5.

### Marker

A **Marker** is not another universal currency. It is an obligation tied to one NPC.

Example: `Alice owes you a Marker.`

A Marker may satisfy a contextual favor request from Alice without spending Leverage. It cannot be transferred to Bob. Markers make relationships matter and support Fixer builds.

## Individual Suspicion

Every relevant NPC tracks player Suspicion from 0–100.

Suggested bands:
- **0–24 Unconcerned** — ordinary behavior.
- **25–49 Attentive** — watches more closely, remembers small anomalies.
- **50–74 Suspicious** — may question, follow, refuse, report, or corroborate concerns.
- **75–99 Convinced** — likely to take active countermeasures.
- **100 Decisive** — takes the strongest contextual action available.

Suspicion is not moral alignment. A murderer can have 0 Suspicion; an innocent investigator can have 90.

### Example deltas

First-balance estimates only:
- Role-appropriate action: +0;
- mild unexplained boundary violation witnessed: +8;
- obvious restricted action witnessed: +18;
- caught in a checkable lie: +15;
- repeatedly absent from expected duty: +5–12;
- witnessed helping NPC: -3 to -8 where socially appropriate;
- credible explanation / leverage intervention: -5 to -15;
- evidence implicating player: +10 to +30 depending on source credibility.

NPC traits and relationships can modify interpretation, not merely multiply numbers.

## Global Exposure

Exposure is also 0–100 but rises more slowly than local Suspicion.

Suggested bands:
- **0–24 Ordinary**
- **25–49 Noticed**
- **50–74 Under Scrutiny**
- **75–99 Compromised**
- **100 Exposed**

Exposure represents a broader social/systemic picture: multiple people talking, records accumulating, security attention, reputation, visible inconsistencies, and the player's identity becoming central to the incident.

At 100, trigger a severe contextual consequence rather than an automatic generic game-over. Examples:
- security detains the player;
- management removes access;
- police focus on the player;
- train staff prevent movement between cars;
- jury foreperson reports misconduct;
- prison staff initiate lockdown/search.

Some Agendas can still be winnable after 100 Exposure.

### Exposure sources

Prototype estimates:
- mild witnessed violation: +2;
- major witnessed violation: +6;
- checkable lie exposed: +5;
- multiple NPCs independently crossing a high Suspicion threshold: +3–8;
- formal record/evidence implicating player: +5–20;
- clearing a credible misunderstanding: -2 to -8;
- successful reputation repair: -5 to -12.

Exposure should decay slowly or only through explicit interventions. It should not behave like a stealth meter that magically resets after hiding behind a wall.

## Social propagation

NPC Suspicion should not instantly synchronize.

Information spreads through believable channels:
- conversation;
- radio;
- formal report;
- shared records;
- witnessing another NPC confront the player;
- location-specific communication systems.

This allows states such as:

`Security Suspicion: 88`

`Front Desk Manager: 22`

`Guest Alice: 7`

The player can then use Leverage or actions to contain, redirect, discredit, or exploit that asymmetry.

---

## Push-your-luck relationship

Exposure should create **opportunity as well as danger** for some builds.

Examples:
- Daredevil pays more above 60 Exposure.
- Nothing to Lose unlocks options above 75.
- certain Milestones require successful resolution at 80–90+.
- high Exposure can cause NPCs to volunteer information, follow the player, or reveal alliances because they believe the situation is reaching a climax.

The goal is to make `high Exposure` a risky build state, not merely a punishment bar.

---

## Open balance questions

1. Should Global Exposure be a true weighted aggregate of social/system state or a separately authored pressure score?
2. How much information about individual Suspicion should be numerically visible versus represented through behavior/portraits/status bands?
3. Should Leverage have a small carry cap to encourage spending?
4. Can the player go into "Leverage debt" for desperation actions, creating future obligations?
5. Which Stakes alter Suspicion/Exposure directly versus changing world rules that indirectly make them harder to manage?

These should be tested in the Hotel prototype rather than answered only on paper.
