# Approaches & Edges

## Purpose

Approaches are persistent skill-tree/playstyle families. **Edges** are permanent unlocks within those trees. The player equips a limited Edge loadout before a run; they do not carry every unlocked ability at once.

Edges should change rules, create options, or reward distinctive behavior. Avoid flat percentage upgrades where possible.

## Loadout target

Prototype: equip **2 Edges**.

Full-game direction: likely **3–5 equipped Edges**, with account progression unlocking Edge points and/or loadout capacity carefully enough to preserve tradeoffs.

Cross-tree builds are encouraged.

## Approach 1 — Observer

Fantasy: notice what others miss; build reliable timelines; distinguish evidence from noise.

Candidate Edges:
- **Corroborate** — when two independent sources support the same fact, mark it corroborated automatically.
- **Remember That** — personally witnessed facts retain precise time/context rather than degrading to approximate notes.
- **Second Look** — revisit one observed scene/object and reveal one contextual detail you could have noticed earlier.
- **Pattern Recognition** — repeated anomalies of the same type become easier to notice across the current run.
- **Read the Room** — entering a populated area reveals who currently appears unusually tense, attentive, or evasive without explaining why.
- **Nothing Gets Past Me** — once per phase, automatically record one relevant environmental change while the player is busy elsewhere nearby.

## Approach 2 — Fixer

Fantasy: relationships, favors, access, obligations, and social problem-solving.

Candidate Edges:
- **Remember That Thing I Did for You?** — after completing a meaningful favor for an NPC, the next eligible Leverage interaction with that NPC is free.
- **Remember That One Time?** — a stronger/later variant that can revive an older favor or relationship condition once per run.
- **Calling in a Favor** — reduce the cost of a contextual NPC request when the player has previously helped that person.
- **Marker** — completing a sufficiently meaningful favor can place a personal Marker on that NPC; cash it in later for one contextual favor without spending fungible Leverage.
- **One-Man Band** — temporarily keep one occupational responsibility covered while the player is away, if a plausible coworker/system can be arranged.
- **I Know a Guy** — the first time a location-specific service/contact is needed, reveal one plausible NPC route to obtaining it.

## Approach 3 — Ghost

Fantasy: belong, blend, move quietly, and avoid becoming the story.

Candidate Edges:
- **Act Natural** — Role-appropriate routine actions cannot generate Global Exposure by themselves.
- **Houdini** — the first time the player is detained, trapped, locked out, or has access revoked, create an escape/recovery opportunity at a significant cost.
- **Out of Sight** — Suspicion decays faster with NPCs who have not seen the player recently.
- **Right Place, Right Time** — entering an area during a plausible duty window reduces the Suspicion generated by being there.
- **Forgettable Face** — low-Suspicion NPCs are slower to connect separate minor incidents to the player.
- **Exit Strategy** — when entering a restricted area, preview one plausible low-Exposure route back out.

## Approach 4 — Operator

Fantasy: exploit schedules, systems, equipment, physical workflows, and location infrastructure.

Candidate Edges:
- **One-Man Band** may alternatively live here or be a Fixer/Operator bridge Edge.
- **Systems Check** — interacting with a location system reveals its normal state and recent obvious anomaly.
- **Keep It Running** — temporary equipment problems can be stabilized without fully resolving them.
- **Routing Table** — location logistics information (rooms, cars, gates, duties) becomes more useful for planning access and movement.
- **Work Smarter** — first Asset that is clearly occupational equipment gains an extra contextual use.
- **Back Door** — once per run, identify an alternate legitimate route into a system/area normally controlled by another Role.

## Approach 5 — Con Artist

Fantasy: deception, misdirection, assumed competence, identity, and interpretation management.

Candidate Edges:
- **Doppelgänger** — after sufficiently observing another Role's routine, temporarily satisfy some of that Role's "belongs here" conditions without actually gaining all privileges.
- **Magician** — manipulating an object while relevant observers are distracted generates less Suspicion.
- **Illusionist** — spend Leverage after a suspicious observation to offer a plausible alternate interpretation; the NPC may accept the explanation without changing the underlying truth.
- **Poker Face** — discovering a contradiction does not automatically signal to the speaker that the player noticed it.
- **Plausible Deniability** — first minor lie contradicted by evidence causes reduced Suspicion if an alternate explanation exists.
- **Confidence Trick** — when presenting a true fact inside a deceptive story, gain a bonus to the story's credibility.

## Approach 6 — Gambler

Fantasy: deliberately court danger, uncertainty, and high Exposure for stronger rewards.

Candidate Edges:
- **Daredevil** — optional high-risk objectives completed above an Exposure threshold pay increased Leverage and meta reward.
- **Double Down** — once per run, voluntarily raise the cost/consequence of an unresolved lead to increase its possible reward.
- **Nothing to Lose** — certain social options improve while Global Exposure is very high.
- **Press Your Luck** — after becoming eligible to resolve, continuing to investigate grants a stacking reward multiplier but increases pressure.
- **Against the Odds** — succeeding at an action while a highly suspicious NPC is watching creates bonus Leverage.
- **All In** — consume most/all current Leverage for one exceptional contextual intervention; failure has an amplified consequence.

## Edge naming direction

Favor flavorful, memorable names that imply behavior and personality:
- Remember That Thing I Did for You?
- Remember That One Time?
- Calling in a Favor
- Marker
- Doppelgänger
- Magician
- Daredevil
- Houdini
- Illusionist
- One-Man Band

These names set a preferred tone: recognizable phrases/archetypes, dark humor where appropriate, and mechanics that players can tell stories about.

## Design constraints

- No Edge should directly identify the culprit or reveal hidden truth.
- Avoid mandatory best-in-slot Edges.
- Cross-tree synergies should be common enough to encourage experimentation.
- Role and Location should alter the usefulness of an Edge without making it dead content.
- Higher Stakes should occasionally make underused Edges attractive rather than only demanding stronger versions of the same build.
- Edge unlocks should come from account progression and specific Milestones, not only from grinding a universal currency.
