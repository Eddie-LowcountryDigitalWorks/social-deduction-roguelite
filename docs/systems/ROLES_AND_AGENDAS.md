# Roles & Agendas

## Principle

**Role** and **Agenda** are separate axes.

A Role answers: *What are you publicly supposed to be doing here?*

An Agenda answers: *What do you privately need to accomplish, protect, hide, discover, or cause?*

The same Role must be able to support multiple Agendas so suspicious behavior cannot be inferred from profession alone.

## Role design

A Role should provide:
- ordinary duties;
- expected locations and routine;
- legitimate access;
- social expectations;
- plausible reasons to interact with people/objects;
- specific consequences for neglecting duties;
- location-specific information channels.

## Agenda families

Agendas are broader than alignment labels such as Innocent/Evil.

### Personal
The player wants something unrelated to the central incident.

Examples:
- protect a reputation;
- retrieve an item;
- help a specific person;
- finish the shift without losing a job;
- conceal an affair/debt/embarrassment;
- reach a destination.

### Investigative
Learn or prove something while maintaining the Role.

Examples:
- determine what happened;
- verify whether a person is lying;
- protect a witness;
- find an object before Resolution.

### Opportunist
Exploit chaos for personal benefit without necessarily causing the incident.

Examples:
- theft;
- blackmail;
- acquire confidential information;
- manipulate an outcome.

### Accomplice / Protector
Help another person accomplish or conceal something.

The player may know the person, the goal, both, or neither completely depending on the scenario.

### Adversarial
Cause or complete the central harmful objective while appearing to fulfill the Role.

Examples:
- murderer;
- saboteur;
- thief central to the incident;
- manipulator/frame artist.

### Scapegoat / Framed
The player's briefing or initial understanding does not reveal that evidence/social pressure is being arranged around them.

This supports self-uncertainty without claiming the player forgot their own actions.

### Infected / Converting
The player may begin infected, become infected, or remain uncertain about infection. The objective can change after confirmed/latent infection.

Digital implementation can allow delayed symptoms, uncertain self-status, and team conversion in ways tabletop games cannot easily reproduce.

### Blind Cell / Unknown Ally
The player knows another participant shares or complements the Agenda but does not know who.

This is primarily a later multiplayer mode, but NPC versions could appear in solo scenarios.

## Role + Agenda examples

- Night Clerk / Personal — keep the hotel functioning while secretly finding who has been using a false name.
- Night Clerk / Scapegoat — perform the shift while unexplained records begin implicating the player.
- Housekeeper / Investigator — identify which room was entered before cleaning without revealing why the player cares.
- Guest / Opportunist — recover confidential documents during an unrelated incident.
- Conductor / Protector — ensure one passenger reaches the final station regardless of suspicion around them.
- Juror / Compromised — participate in deliberation while concealing an improper outside contact.
- Juror / Personal — the player has a private piece of context relevant to the case but no malicious Agenda.
- Passenger / Infected — continue normal travel while discovering whether and how the condition spreads.

## Self-knowledge rules

The game may hide or distort **context**, but should be careful about hiding actions the player personally performed.

Good uncertainty:
- the briefing's source is unreliable;
- the player was framed;
- the player unknowingly transported an object;
- an ally is not who they claim;
- infection status is latent;
- an ordinary mistake became central to the incident;
- the player's personal objective is part of someone else's plan.

Bad uncertainty:
- pretending the player committed major deliberate acts offscreen without setup;
- arbitrary memory loss used only to create a twist;
- withholding information the player character obviously should know unless the fiction explicitly supports it.

## Multiplayer direction — deferred

Potential modes once solo simulation works:
- Conspiracy — 1 vs many;
- Partners — 2 conspirators who know each other;
- Blind Cell — 2 allied conspirators who do not initially know each other;
- Crossed Wires — several unknown small teams;
- Everyone for Themselves — only personal Agendas;
- Masquerade — human players hidden among NPC population;
- Infected — team membership changes during play.

Multiplayer should reuse Roles, Agendas, Suspicion, Exposure, Leverage, Assets, and Stakes rather than becoming a separate ruleset.
