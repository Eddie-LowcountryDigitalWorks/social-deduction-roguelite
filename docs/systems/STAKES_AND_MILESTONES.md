# Raise the Stakes & Milestones

## Raise the Stakes

**Stakes** are optional difficulty modifiers selected before a run. They are not the same as in-run Exposure.

The player deliberately chooses complications, each worth a Stake value. Higher total Stakes increase progression rewards and enable special Milestones/unlocks.

This should create the feeling: **"I can probably handle one more complication."**

## Modifier principles

Good Stakes:
- alter rules or information;
- change how a familiar system must be approached;
- create new combinations of pressure;
- preserve solvability;
- sometimes make different Edge builds more attractive.

Bad Stakes:
- only inflate numbers;
- make NPCs universally faster/smarter with no new decision;
- remove required evidence and turn deduction into guessing;
- create unavoidable failure.

## Candidate Stakes

### General
- **Bad Intel +2** — one element of the starting briefing may be incomplete or unreliable.
- **Crossed Motives +1** — add another unrelated secret Agenda/scheme to the cast.
- **Sharp Memories +1** — NPCs retain witnessed suspicious behavior longer.
- **Planted Evidence +3** — the incident may include deliberately fabricated evidence; provenance becomes more important.
- **No Second Chances +2** — one category of social failure cannot be immediately retried.
- **Thin Ice +1** — the run begins with slightly elevated institutional attention, not simply raw Exposure.
- **Short Clock +2** — reduce time before Resolution.
- **Closed Ranks +2** — one social group is harder to influence without first earning trust.
- **Loose Lips +1** — NPC information spreads more quickly between connected characters, for better or worse.
- **Competing Crisis +2** — add a secondary operational problem that consumes Role time/resources.

### Hotel-specific
- **Skeleton Crew +1** — fewer staff are available to cover duties.
- **Fully Booked +1** — more movement/requests and fewer empty rooms create denser noise.
- **Management Is Watching +2** — neglecting Night Clerk duties has stronger consequences.

### Train-specific
- **Short Stops +2** — station windows are shorter.
- **Express Service +1** — fewer stops, therefore fewer opportunities to recover from mistakes.
- **Car Closed +1** — one route/access path is intermittently unavailable.

### Jury-specific
- **Sequestered +1** — fewer outside opportunities to verify context.
- **Objection Sustained +2** — more information is formally excluded, increasing the importance of private observations and provenance.

## Rewards

Higher Stakes should improve:
- account mastery/progression rate;
- chance/access to rarer unlock conditions;
- Milestone eligibility;
- special Asset/Agenda pools;
- campaign discoveries where appropriate.

Do not make campaign completion require grinding the absolute highest Stakes unless that is explicitly an endgame challenge path.

## Milestones

Milestones are specific accomplishments. Some can map directly to platform achievements later, but their game-design purpose is to reward experimentation and unlock meaningful possibilities.

### Design rule

Prefer:
> Do an interesting thing → unlock an interesting possibility.

Avoid:
> Grind a large universal currency → buy arbitrary content.

## Candidate Milestones

- **Perfectly Normal** — complete a Hotel run without exceeding 20 Exposure. Unlock a Ghost Edge or stealth-oriented Asset.
- **Everyone Thinks It's Me** — successfully resolve while above 90 Exposure. Unlock a high-risk Gambler Edge.
- **What Could Go Wrong?** — complete any run at Stakes 5+. Unlock first advanced Agenda/Stake option.
- **Against Better Judgment** — become eligible to resolve, deliberately continue investigating, and improve the final conclusion. Unlock push-your-luck content.
- **Last Call** — complete a Train run after remaining aboard through an optional high-risk stop window.
- **By the Book** — complete a Role-heavy run while never entering an area outside legitimate access. Unlock a Role/Operator Edge.
- **Not What It Looks Like** — convince a highly suspicious NPC of a plausible alternate interpretation without falsifying the underlying evidence. Unlock Illusionist.
- **I Owe You One** — finish a run with multiple unspent personal Markers. Unlock an advanced Fixer Edge.
- **Now You See Me** — escape a high-restriction state and still complete the Agenda. Unlock Houdini.
- **One-Man Band** — complete all required occupational duties while also completing an optional incident objective. Unlock One-Man Band.

## Account mastery

A broad account progression track is still needed for Edge points/loadout growth and general progression pacing.

Working-name candidates:
- Acuity
- Instinct
- Expertise
- Vigilance
- Sleuth (too detective-specific)
- Inquisition (strong flavor but wrong connotations/meaning for the broad system)

The name is intentionally not locked.

## Progression mix

The current preferred structure is:
- **Account mastery** → Edge points / careful loadout progression / broad progression gates.
- **Milestones** → specific Edges, Assets, Agendas, Stakes, cosmetics, or scenario possibilities.
- **Raise the Stakes** → voluntary difficulty and reward multiplier/challenge structure.
- **Case Files / campaign discoveries** → authored narrative progression and location/story unlocks.

This mix should keep long-term goals worthwhile without turning every system into another currency.
