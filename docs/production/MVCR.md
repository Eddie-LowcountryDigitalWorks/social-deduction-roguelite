# Hotel MVCR — Minimum Viable Complete Run

## Purpose

Prove that the game is fun as a complete 8–12 minute solo run before adding large content pools, multiplayer, or additional locations.

The MVCR must demonstrate the loop:

**ordinary role → social baseline → complication → incident → build/economy choices → push-your-luck escalation → resolution → meta reward → desire for another run**

## Scope

### Location
One compact hotel containing:
- lobby / front desk;
- one guest-room corridor with 6–8 rooms;
- small staff/service area;
- one restricted office or security room;
- elevator/stair access represented simply.

### Player Role
**Night Clerk** only.

Expected duties give the player plausible reasons to move and interact:
- check in a guest;
- issue/replace a key;
- respond to a room request;
- deliver or arrange one service item;
- answer a guest/staff question;
- remain available at the desk often enough that absence can matter.

### Approaches
Two available at prototype scale:
- **Observer** — evidence, contradictions, memory, surveillance;
- **Fixer** — favors, access, relationships, Leverage efficiency.

Player equips **2 Edges** before the run.

### NPC population
Target **8 named NPCs**:
- 4 guests;
- 3 staff/contractors;
- 1 security/management NPC.

Every NPC needs:
- name and visual identity;
- Role;
- simple routine/schedule;
- one relationship or contextual tie;
- at least one fact they know;
- at least one thing they want;
- optional secret unrelated to the central incident;
- individual Suspicion toward the player.

### Agendas
MVCR needs at least three player Agenda variants:
1. **Ordinary / Personal Goal** — perform duties plus a private non-criminal objective.
2. **Investigator / Curious** — identify what happened or protect a person while maintaining cover.
3. **Scapegoat / Framed** — the player initially lacks full knowledge that events are being arranged to implicate them.

A direct murderer Agenda is intentionally deferred until the underlying social simulation is proven.

### Incident families
At least **2 truth-first incident templates**, each with variable participants:
- theft / disappearance / unauthorized access;
- suspicious injury or death presented abstractly, without combat mechanics.

Each run also generates **1 unrelated secret/scheme** so lying does not equal guilt.

## Run phases

### 0:00–2:00 — Routine
Player performs normal Night Clerk work, learns faces, and sees baseline NPC behavior.

### 2:00–4:00 — Complication
A complaint, unusual request, missing item, key request, argument, unexplained absence, or access anomaly occurs.

### 4:00–7:00 — Incident
The central problem becomes clear enough to demand attention. Player gains opportunities to investigate or manipulate while duties continue.

### 7:00–10:00 — Escalation
Time pressure rises. Assets and Edges should now create a recognizable run build. Exposure and local Suspicion matter. Player chooses whether to resolve or keep digging.

### 10:00–12:00 — Resolution
Dawn / manager arrival / police arrival / shift handoff forces an outcome.

## Initial Assets
Target 8–10 temporary Assets:
- Pocket Notebook
- Pocket Recorder
- Staff Radio
- Master / Service Key
- Guest Ledger
- Security Camera Access
- Lost-and-Found Access
- Staff Uniform / Jacket
- Disposable Phone
- Favor with a named NPC

Not every Asset appears every run.

## Initial synergy examples

1. **Pocket Recorder + Observer Edge: Corroborate**
   - recorded statements can automatically flag contradictions with known facts.

2. **Staff Radio + Fixer Edge: Remember That Thing I Did for You?**
   - a Marker owed by staff can be redeemed remotely over radio when context permits.

3. **Service Key + Act Natural-style access Edge**
   - first appropriate service-area entry creates no Exposure if the player is plausibly performing a duty.

4. **Guest Ledger + Pocket Notebook**
   - observed room/time facts can be checked against official occupancy records.

5. **Security Camera Access + Guest Ledger**
   - matching identity/location records can lower uncertainty but never automatically identify the culprit.

## Leverage baseline

Start: **1 Leverage**.

Typical gains:
- complete a meaningful duty: +1;
- do a meaningful favor: +1 and possibly create a Marker;
- complete optional risky objective: +2;
- discover/useful contradiction with qualifying Edge: +1;
- voluntarily operate at high Exposure during a risk event: bonus +1 where appropriate.

Typical costs:
- small request / minor cooperation: 1;
- restricted information / meaningful favor: 2;
- temporary access / strong intervention: 3;
- major exception to normal rules: 4–5.

Golden rule: **Leverage creates access, cooperation, opportunity, or interpretation; it never reveals the truth directly.**

## Suspicion / Exposure baseline

Each NPC tracks Suspicion from 0–100.

Suggested bands:
- 0–24: unconcerned;
- 25–49: attentive;
- 50–74: actively suspicious;
- 75–99: hostile / likely to report, follow, refuse, or confront;
- 100: certain enough to take decisive contextual action.

Global Exposure is also 0–100 and changes more slowly.

Suggested bands:
- 0–24: ordinary;
- 25–49: noticed;
- 50–74: under scrutiny;
- 75–99: compromised;
- 100: exposed — triggers a severe consequence, not necessarily immediate run loss.

Example event deltas for first balance pass:
- ordinary Role-appropriate action: +0;
- mild unexplained boundary violation witnessed: +8 local Suspicion, +2 Exposure;
- obvious restricted action witnessed: +18 local, +6 Exposure;
- caught lying about a checkable fact: +15 local, +5 Exposure;
- credible explanation / leverage intervention: reduce local Suspicion 5–15;
- being independently implicated by evidence: +10–25 Exposure depending on severity.

Exposure should be partially recoverable but difficult to erase completely.

## Push-your-luck decision

The MVCR must contain a clear moment where the player can resolve now or continue.

Example:
- current interpretation has 70–80% confidence;
- personal objective is complete or nearly complete;
- Resolution is approaching;
- another lead is available but requires entering a restricted area, neglecting duties, spending Leverage, or risking contact with a highly suspicious NPC.

Continuing should offer a meaningful reward: better outcome rating, extra meta progression, a Milestone condition, rarer campaign discovery, or protection against a false conclusion.

## Resolution scoring dimensions

Do not reduce the outcome to "correct culprit / wrong culprit."

Score/grade separately:
- Role duties completed;
- Agenda objective completed;
- central incident understanding;
- collateral harm / reputation consequences;
- Exposure at Resolution;
- optional discoveries;
- risk taken / Stakes when later enabled.

## Meta reward in the MVCR

One complete run must visibly produce something persistent.

Prototype example:
- account mastery progress (working name Acuity / Instinct);
- one Edge point after a threshold;
- at least one Milestone capable of unlocking a new Edge or Asset into the future pool.

The reward screen should make the player understand **why another run can be meaningfully different**.

## MVCR success criteria

The build is not successful merely because all systems function.

A successful MVCR produces these player reactions:
1. "I had a theory about what was happening."
2. "At least one innocent-looking or guilty-looking person surprised me."
3. "My Edge/Asset combination changed what I could do."
4. "I made a deliberate risk/reward decision near the end."
5. "I understand why I succeeded or failed."
6. "The persistent reward gives me a concrete reason to run again."
7. "I want to know what would have happened with another Agenda or incident."
